# 高冲击人物科技封面

Use this style for technology, AI-tool, product-practice, and strong judgment topics that need fast recognition in a feed. It abstracts reusable composition principles from creator thumbnails; it must not imitate a specific creator.

## Information hierarchy

1. Use one 4–10 Chinese-character main hook, normally no more than two lines. It carries the conflict, question, or result promised by the approved content.
2. Add at most one short eyebrow or support line. Keep readable text to two groups.
3. Use one trust or topic anchor: an approved creator portrait, or a product icon, interface fragment, device, diagram, or other concrete topic object.
4. Limit stickers, arrows, question marks, or small cards to one or two useful accents. Remove decorations that compete with the headline.

## Visual system

- Use a dark or very light base, high-contrast heavy type, and exactly one saturated accent family such as red, electric blue, cyan, lime, or violet.
- The main headline should occupy roughly 35–55% of the visible area and remain readable at 320 px width.
- Separate foreground from background with a restrained outline, drop shadow, glow, or rim light. Do not use all effects at once.
- Build three depth layers: energetic but simple background, topic asset, then headline or portrait. Avoid generic robot heads, neon brains, dense circuitry, and meaningless tiny text.
- The visual promise must match the approved script. Strong tension is allowed; fabricated outcomes and anxiety bait are not.

## Portrait safety and fallback

- Use a person only when the caller provides an approved creator image and explicitly authorizes it for the cover.
- Preserve the creator's identity. Do not substitute, synthesize, beautify into a different person, or infer appearance from account text.
- Prefer a clean cutout, clear gesture or expression, and a restrained white or accent-color rim light. The portrait normally occupies 30–45% of the canvas.
- If no approved portrait exists, use `topic-object` fallback automatically. Make a product icon, UI fragment, device, workflow node, or symbolic object the focal subject. Do not generate a random presenter.

## Independent layouts

### 16:9

- Place the headline in a 44–52% left or right zone and the portrait or focal object in the opposite 34–43% zone.
- Keep horizontal safe margins near 6% and vertical margins near 7%.
- One oversized word may approach an edge for energy, but every required character stays inside the safe area.

### 4:3

- Recompose the headline into the upper-left or upper half and place the portrait or object lower-right or centrally.
- Keep all edges near 7% safe margin. Reduce supporting assets before shrinking the headline.

### 3:4

- Put the headline in the upper 35–43% and the portrait or focal object in the middle-lower section.
- Keep top and side margins near 8% and reserve the bottom 12% for platform overlays.
- Do not place the support line or important face details at the bottom.

## Final checks

- The eye reaches headline first, portrait or focal object second, supporting topic cue third.
- The main hook is readable at 320 px width and does not exceed two lines.
- There is one accent color family and no more than two decorative accents.
- Any portrait is the caller-approved creator asset; otherwise the topic-object fallback was used.
- The result does not reproduce the reference creator's face, wording, logo, or signature composition.
