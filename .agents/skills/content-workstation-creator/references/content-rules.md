# Content rules

## Voice

- Write from a calm, practical practitioner perspective.
- Use short spoken sentences and concrete transitions such as “我当时先看的是…” or “这里真正要判断的是…”, only when supported by the creator context.
- Explain technical terms through what the product manager sees, decides, or verifies.
- Avoid formulaic phrases such as “在当今时代”, “赋能”, “颠覆”, “首先其次最后”, or repeated rhetorical questions.

## Structure

1. Open with a work conflict or consequence within the first 30 seconds.
2. State why the common approach fails.
3. Give the creator's decision principle.
4. Break the method into observable checkpoints.
5. Close with one action the viewer can take immediately.

For mixed recording, make the opening and conclusion suitable for on-camera delivery. Make the body suitable for an HTML screen recording, with visible sections matching the spoken progression.

## Evidence boundary

Classify material mentally before writing:

- **Provided fact:** may be stated directly.
- **Reasonable professional judgment:** frame as analysis or recommendation, not personal history.
- **Missing case detail:** ask for it in `missingCaseQuestions`.
- **Unsupported metric or outcome:** omit it.

Never turn a generic industry pattern into “我的真实项目”.

## Platform adaptation

- Bilibili: searchable, explanatory title and a structured description.
- Xiaohongshu: concise benefit-led title, scannable description, practical topic tags.
- WeChat Channels: credible conclusion first, restrained title, discussion-oriented close.
- Douyin: immediate conflict and payoff, compact description, strong but truthful opening.

Keep claims truthful across all platforms.
